Support

How to get help

Use the contact option shown for Veysel Baysal on the Advanced Structured Gantt page in Atlassian Marketplace. Include your Jira Cloud site URL, the app version, the affected view and a short description of the result you expected.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm the JQL query returns work items in Jira.
  • Check the configured start, end and story point fields in Settings.
  • Confirm that your Jira account can view the affected work items.
  • For date changes, confirm that your account can edit the configured date fields.
  • For export issues, include the selected format, scope and browser.

Languages

Support requests may be submitted in English or Turkish.
